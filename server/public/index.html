The overlay

Maple Classic Exp Overlay

Know exactly how fast you're leveling, without doing any math.

  • EXP/hr and time to your next level
    Over your whole session and the last 5 minutes.
  • Mesos/hr
    Read from your inventory, even while it's see-through in fights.

Keeping the numbers honest

Anyone could send made-up numbers, so nothing is taken on trust.

15 minutes non-stop

Each run is 15 minutes of non-stop farming on one map with the same party. Breaks don't count.

One player, one vote

Each player counts once (their typical run), however much they play.

Outliers left out

Runs under half or over double what others get on that map are dropped.

3 players or more

A map only shows once at least 3 different players have farmed it.

Questions

What gets shared?

Only 15-minute runs of non-stop farming, and only if you said yes in the overlay: the map, when you farmed it, your level, job and party size, the EXP and mesos you got, and a random ID for your copy of the overlay. Never your character's name. You can stop sharing any time in the overlay's settings.

Why is Path greyed out?

Under Lv 30 you're still 1st job, and everyone plays the same until they pick their 2nd job, so you see what every player of your class gets around your level. Path opens at Lv 30. From then on, each path only counts its own jobs: Shadower is Bandits, Chief Bandits and Shadowers, for example.

Is the overlay safe?

It only looks at your screen, like a screen recorder. It never touches the game's files or memory and never presses keys or clicks for you. It's open source, so anyone can check. It isn't made by or affiliated with Nexon, so like any third-party tool, you use it at your own risk.

Does it cost anything?

No. The overlay and this site are free, made by a player for the community.

Why isn't my map or class here yet?

A map shows up once 3 different players around your level (within 5 levels), on the same path, and solo or in a party like you, have shared a run there. The more people share, the faster it fills in. You can help by sharing yours!
